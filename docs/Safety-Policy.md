# Safety Policy

## Product boundary

InnerArc is reflective wellness content, not medical care, mental-health diagnosis, legal advice, financial advice, emergency response, or crime assistance.

## Prohibited claims

No exact future prediction, accuracy percentage, guaranteed success, mandated breakup, betrayal accusation, accident warning, investment directive, or mental-health diagnosis. Compatibility is never destiny.

## High-risk routing

- **Medical:** encourage qualified care and evidence-based information; symbols may only frame feelings or questions.
- **Legal:** encourage a qualified local professional and document/fact review.
- **Financial/investment:** encourage risk, affordability, time horizon, and regulated advice checks; never name a trade.
- **Self-harm or imminent violence:** stop the reading flow, encourage immediate local emergency/crisis support and contact with a trusted person, and keep language direct and compassionate.
- **Crime:** refuse facilitation and redirect toward safety/lawful options.

Locale-specific crisis resources must be current and location-aware before release; do not hard-code a global number as universally valid.

## UX protections

No fear paywall, guilt streak, hidden renewal, hard cancellation, result-gated invite, sensitive pre-result ad, or automatic public sharing. The user can skip optional questions and retains decision ownership.

## Relationship-specific protections

- Do not claim where or when the user will meet a partner, that marriage is certain, or that a described person is destined.
- Do not infer a future partner’s protected traits, health, fidelity, wealth, nationality, exact occupation, or appearance.
- Do not turn “meeting context” relevance into a probability percentage.
- Do not advise pursuit where consent, workplace boundaries, power imbalance, or safety is uncertain.
- Encourage reciprocal interest, consistent behavior, clear boundaries, and observable compatibility over symbolic fit.

## Escalation and review

Safety classifier category, policy version, and action are auditable without retaining raw sensitive text. False positives/negatives are reviewed through redacted cases. Policy changes require regression tests.
