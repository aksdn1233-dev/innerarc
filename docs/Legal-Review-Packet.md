# Legal and Privacy Review Packet

Status: pre-release working packet; not legal advice and not approved launch text.  
Last updated: 2026-07-23

## Review objective

Approve or revise the localized privacy information, terms, consent design, age approach, safety escalation, cross-border processing, subscription disclosures, and data-rights operation before any public launch. Product code must not replace qualified counsel or the named business owner.

## Product facts for counsel

- Product position: symbolic self-reflection and personal-pattern intelligence, not scientific prediction, diagnosis, therapy, legal advice, or investment advice.
- Initial languages: Korean and English. Language does not establish user country or legal jurisdiction.
- Current preview: deterministic calculations and draws run locally; onboarding inputs remain in memory. Tarot history, Reality Checks, and preferences enter browser storage only after explicit device-storage actions.
- Planned production data: account identity, birth date, optional name/romanization, time zone, consents, questions, decisions, outcomes, relationship data supplied by the user, AI run metadata, entitlements, and audit records.
- Separate choices: AI personalization, model training, raw-journal retention, product analytics, and marketing.
- Third-party data: another person's birth date/name may be entered for a private comparison. It is not searchable, public, or reusable without a separate save choice.
- Automated output: numerology and tarot facts are code-generated; AI may interpret canonical facts but cannot change them or select cards.
- Safety: medical, legal, financial, self-harm, violence, and crime content is routed away from definitive symbolic advice.

## Provider/DPA questions

For every auth, database, AI, payment, analytics, monitoring, email, and hosting processor, record legal entity, purpose, data fields, region, retention, training use, encryption, subprocessors, transfer mechanism, deletion SLA, breach notice, audit evidence, and contract/DPA owner. Reject any provider configuration that requires raw questions or journals in analytics or error telemetry.
