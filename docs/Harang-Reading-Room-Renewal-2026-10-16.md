service routes. Existing server-authoritative prices remain unchanged, including the
9,600/39,000 KRW operational price settings and the 39,000/79,000 KRW catalog where
configured. No new provider call, token cost, fulfillment cost, product or recurring fee
is introduced. Existing payment verification and merchant launch gates stay intact.
This change has no migration, new external data recipient or new raw-input logging.

Use the existing opt-in funnel telemetry: landing, free start, completed birth input,
concern category, free result, paid teaser, product selection and checkout. The Saju
surface is added to the closed-set schema; names, birth dates and questions are excluded.

rates, rather than a claimed uplift. Guardrails are unchanged calculation evidence,
consent, payment/entitlement correctness, accessibility, payload budgets and error rates.
