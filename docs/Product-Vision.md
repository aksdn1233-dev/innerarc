# Product Vision

## Positioning

**AI Self-Discovery & Personal Pattern Intelligence Platform**

InnerArc helps people describe how they think, choose, relate, lead, work, handle money, respond to stress, and grow. Numerology and tarot are treated as symbolic languages for reflection. AI connects those symbols to user-supplied context, while later Reality Check reviews separate patterns that felt relevant from patterns that did not.

## Promise

AI does not merely interpret numbers and cards. It remembers choices and real outcomes—with explicit consent—so future reflections can become more personally relevant.

## Users

1. People aged 18–34 exploring identity, relationships, and career.
2. Existing tarot/numerology users who value methods, meanings, evidence, and history.
3. Journaling and personal-growth users who value emotional organization, decisions, actions, and recurring-pattern reports.

## Differentiator: Reality Check Loop

Question → symbolic reflection → reality checks → decision and action → scheduled outcome review → user-rated personal relevance → future context → monthly pattern report.

The product measures **Personal Relevance**, **Pattern Fit**, **Outcome Review**, and **Reflection Match**. It never markets “prediction accuracy.”

## Experience principles

- One primary action per screen.
- Warm, calm, premium wellness tone; strong information hierarchy.
- Evidence is available on demand, not forced into the main flow.
- No fear paywalls, guilt streaks, manipulative subscriptions, or deterministic relationship verdicts.
- The user retains decision authority.
- Lifestyle suggestions make a result tangible through optional style and sound experiments; they never turn an object into luck, protection, healing, or a required purchase.

## Delivery order

The responsive website and installable PWA are the canonical first product. Native iOS/Android work starts only after the web product proves activation, Reality Check return behavior, accessibility, safety, deletion, and unit economics. All later clients reuse the same calculation, safety, evidence, and data-rights contracts rather than forking product truth.

## Success

The leading signal is completed Reality Check outcome reviews, followed by onboarding completion, first-result reach, saved results, D7/D30 retention, and helpfulness. Acquisition or raw reading volume cannot substitute for repeated, outcome-linked use.
