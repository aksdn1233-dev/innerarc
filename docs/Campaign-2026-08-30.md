## Review draw and privacy

- Entry requires a completed paid report, a review submitted during the window, and a separate
  campaign-entry checkbox. Public-review consent is independent and is not required.
- One entry is possible per order. Cancelled, refunded, duplicated, abusive, late, or non-consented
  records are excluded before the draw.
- Sharing is an easy, explicit user action but not an eligibility condition because the application
  does not track or store whether an external app completed delivery.
- Entry adds a versioned server-authored campaign code and consent timestamp to the existing
  operator-only review audit note. It requires no migration and adds no birth date, question,
  report body, email, phone number, address, recipient, or external-share result.
- Use a cryptographically secure random selection from the sorted eligible review IDs. Record the
  query window, eligible count, selected review ID, masked order number, selection timestamp, and
  operator. Publish only the masked order number after the scheduled draw on 2026-09-08 18:00 KST.
- The winner contacts support within seven days and proves access to the report. Delivery details
  are requested only after a separate fulfilment notice and consent; if no valid claim arrives,
  repeat the documented draw from the remaining eligible set.

## Measurement and reversal

- Success measures: verified non-refunded campaign orders by product, checkout completion, report
  readiness, eligible-review count, user-initiated share attempts only where analytics consent
  already exists, and incremental traffic/conversion against the prior seven days.
- Guardrails: payment mismatch 0, negative or stacked discount 0, failed ready-report rate below 1%,
  cross-user/privacy incidents 0, misleading-promotion complaints 0, and support/refund rates no
  worse than the prior paid cohort. Raw birth dates, contact details, report text, and share targets
  never enter campaign analytics.
- Revert campaign presentation and pricing to `a39e80a` if a guardrail fails, while preserving
  historical 1,500 KRW payment verification, valid review-entry evidence, issued coupon rights, and
  the prize obligation. Remove the campaign-specific stylesheet, popup, and utility links after the
  claim window; retain only the minimum draw audit required by the approved retention decision.
