## Escalation rules

1. Cross-user exposure, imminent safety harm, or irreversible deletion/billing integrity failure can trigger the relevant kill switch without waiting for a routine release meeting.
2. Never paste raw questions, journals, birth dates, names, access tokens, or payment data into tickets or chat. Use opaque references and masked evidence.
3. Security/privacy incidents preserve audit metadata, stop the affected path, and follow the legally approved notification assessment.
4. Crisis-resource content has a named verifier, official source, last-verified date, and scheduled refresh. Language is never used as a location signal.
5. Every production incident records detection, scope, user impact, containment, recovery, notification decision, root cause, and prevention test.
